Lines starting with `#` in the CSV files are comments that identify the file and its columns.
Times: BTJD = BJD$`_\mathrm{TDB}`$ $`-`$ 2457000. TESScut time stamps are used as delivered; their barycentric correction refers to the FFI rather than to each target, which shifts individual mid-times by up to about a minute (15-44 s for TIC 311179599), well below the quoted timing errors. Predicted UTC times are geocentric except for the TOI-6482 script's explicitly labelled barycentric values, which convert the time scale without removing the barycentric light-travel correction. TESS mid-transit times come from TESScut cutouts or SPOC light curves, except those of TOI-6482.01, which use QLP light curves; its separate TRAPPIST-South row comes from the observer-provided reduction v2
(section [7](7_caveats.md) on the QLP timing notice). For TIC 89247735 two transits fall in sector 93; its tables label each transit
by its epoch (cycle number) as well as its sector. For TIC 455017950 the epoch is the cycle number relative to the
sector-99 transit, for TIC 311179599 relative to the sector-100 transit, and for its candidate c relative to the
sector-100 event (for both allowed periods).
